# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Controller

Responsible for data processing in the sense of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is {name}, {street}, {city}, {country}, email: {email}.

## 2. The game in the browser / Telegram Mini App

World War 3 runs in your browser or as a Telegram Mini App. Game progress, settings, your avatar and career progress are stored in the local storage of your device and are not transmitted to us; exceptions are your daily streak for the reminders (section 6) and your result when opening a game from a group (section 8). Saving is absolutely required for the function you wish.

When opening the game outside Telegram, the website is hosted by Vercel Inc. (440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA). On access, Vercel processes technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested file, browser type) to deliver the page and protect it from attacks (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f GDPR). Vercel is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; additional standard contractual clauses apply.

Opening the game as a Telegram Mini App, we receive signed start data from Telegram. From this we process your Telegram user ID, first name, username and language setting to recognise you and assign your purchases (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b GDPV). The start data itself is not stored.

## 3. Registration via Telegram

If you write to the bot, we process your messages and your Telegram user ID to answer your requests (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b and f GDPR). Messages are transmitted via Telegram's servers, which may be located outside the EU.

## 4. Purchases with Telegram Stars

For purchases we process your Telegram user ID, the purchased item, the price in Stars, the time, Telegram's payment identifiers, your consent for immediate delivery (with date and version of the terms) as well as your balance of purchased revival tokens, purchased looks and Pro term duration. Legal basis is contractual performance (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b GDPR) and, where storage obligations exist, Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. c GDPR. The payment is handled by Telegram. We do not receive payment data such as card numbers. For processing by Telegram, Telegram's privacy policy applies: https://telegram.org/privacy

## 5. The Telegram Bot

If you write to the bot, we process your messages and your Telegram user ID to answer your requests (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b and f GDPR). Messages are transmitted via Telegram's servers, which may be located outside the EU.

## 6. Daily streak reminders

If you play in Telegram, the game reports to the bot the length of your series at the daily reward and the time at which it ends (midnight according to your device time). Two hours beforehand the bot sends you a reminder, provided it may write to you. For this we store your Telegram user ID, the series length, this time, your language (German or English) and whether you have disabled the reminders. Each new message overwrites the previous one. Legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b GDPR (the reminder is part of the game you are using). You can disable the reminders at any time with the button in the message or with /reminders.

## 7. Invitation links

Every player receives a personal invitation link that contains their Telegram user ID in encoded form. If someone who has never used the bot starts the bot via this link, we store who invited whom and reward the inviting person with a reward; they receive a message without the name of the invited person. Legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b GDPR (the reward is part of the game). We keep this as long as the credited revival tokens count in the balance, and we delete them on request.

## 8. Leaderboards in groups

If you add the bot to a group, it can post games there. If you press Play in such a game, we store a game session with your Telegram user ID, your first name, your language setting, the game, the group and the message so that your result appears in the leaderboard of exactly that group; after a day it is deleted. At the end of a round the game reports your result. If it is your new best performance in that group, we update the leaderboard of the game with your name and result. We store your best performance per game and group with your first name so that /top can display the group's leaderboard; all members of the group can see these entries. Legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b GDPR (the leaderboard is part of the game you open from a group). If you do not open the game from a group, nothing of this is stored.

## 9. First-party advertising

The game shows only first-party advertising, i.e. hints on our own channels and functions. No advertising network is included and no cookies are set. The current advertisements are fetched from our own advertisement interface, so your browser does not access it directly. If you see an advertisement, tap on it or watch it to the end, the game only reports the advertisement's identifier and the type of event. Day totals per advertisement are formed from this. Your IP address, your Telegram user ID or other data about you are not passed on or stored in the process. Legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f GDPR (our legitimate interest in finding out how well our hints are received).

## 10. Hosting of the bot, the interface and the database

The bot, the interface via which the Mini App queries your account, purchases and series, and the database with accounts, purchases, consents, series and leaderboards run on a server we operate ourselves. Connections to the interface are forwarded via Tailscale Funnel of Tailscale Inc. (Canada); Tailscale thereby sees your IP address and the time, the contents remain encrypted (https://tailscale.com/privacy-policy). For Canada there is an adequacy decision of the EU Commission. Legal basis is Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b and f GDPR (error logs contain technical details (time, error message, if applicable your Telegram user ID) and serve for error correction).

## 11. Storage period

Daily streak data and your entries in leaderboards we delete on request immediately; game sessions we delete after one day. Purchase and consent data we keep as long as you use the game, and beyond that as long as statutory storage obligations require (up to ten years per § 147 AO and § 257 HGB). Account data without purchases we delete on request immediately.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your place of residence.

## 13. No automated decision-making

Automated decision-making including profiling does not take place.
