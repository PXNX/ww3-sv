# Right of withdrawal

Consumers have a right of withdrawal subject to the following provisions. A consumer is any natural person who concludes a contract for purposes that are predominantly not related to their commercial or self-employed professional activity.

## The right of withdrawal

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within fourteen days without giving a reason.

The withdrawal period is fourteen days from the day the contract was concluded.

To exercise your right of withdrawal, you must inform us ({name}, {street}, {city}, {country}, E-mail: {email}) by means of a clear declaration (for example a letter sent by post or an email) that you intend to withdraw from this contract. You may use the attached withdrawal form, which is not mandatory.

For the purpose of complying with the withdrawal period, it is sufficient to send your notification of the exercise of the right of withdrawal before the end of the period.

## Consequences of the withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we will refund all payments we have received from you immediately and at the latest within fourteen days from the day on which we received your notification of the withdrawal. For this refund we use the same payment means you used for the original transaction (Telegram Stars), unless you expressly agreed to something else; in no case shall you be charged for this refund.

If you requested that the service (World War 3 Pro) begin within the withdrawal period, you shall pay us an appropriate amount corresponding to the proportion of the service already provided up to the time you informed us of your exercise of the withdrawal right with regard to this contract, compared to the total scope of services offered in the contract.

## Early expiry of the right of withdrawal

For contracts for the provision of digital content (revival tokens, looks for the agent), the right of withdrawal expires once we have begun performance after you

- expressly agreed that we begin performance before the end of the withdrawal period,
- confirmed that you thereby lose your right of withdrawal when performance begins, and
- that we have provided you with confirmation of the contract on a durable medium (message in the Telegram chat).

The digital content is credited immediately after the payment. Before every purchase we ask for your consent and confirmation.

For World War 3 Pro, the right of withdrawal expires when we have fully performed the service and have begun performance after you expressly consented to it and confirmed at the same time that you lose your right of withdrawal upon full performance.
